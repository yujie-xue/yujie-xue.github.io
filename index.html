01 About

Development, institutions
and economic change.

I am an economics researcher interested in how institutions, productive capabilities and public policy shape development.

My research interests span development economics, economic history, political economy and industrial development, with a particular focus on China and Africa.

My experience includes public finance research at the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA), research assistance in economic history and political economy at the London School of Economics and Political Science, and sustainability research at the Paulson Institute.

I hold a master’s degree in economics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a bachelor’s degree in economics from The University of Queensland.
